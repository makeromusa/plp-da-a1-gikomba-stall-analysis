# Gikomba Market Stall Analysis

**Author:** Asman Makero Musa · PLP Data Analytics Cohort 7  

## Business Problem
Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market. She recorded daily sales in a notebook for 14 days and tracked inventory across five commodities (Tomatoes, Onions, Sukuma, Potatoes, and Avocado). She hired an analyst to evaluate stall performance, calculate week-on-week growth, identify stock needing reorder, and determine where to focus to improve revenue.

## How to Run
Open `stall_analysis.ipynb` in Google Colab and select **Runtime → Run all**:

## Top Three Findings
1. **Total Sales & Growth**: Stall revenue totaled **KES 46,450.00** over 14 days, with sales growing **10.66%** from Week 1 (KES 22,050.00) to Week 2 (KES 24,400.00).
2. **Weekend Sales Surge**: Weekend sales averaged **KES 4,900.00**, which is **82.50% higher** than the weekday average of **KES 2,685.00**, peaking on Saturday of Week 2 at **KES 5,600.00**.
3. **Critical Stock Reorders**: Two commodities have fallen below safety stock levels: **Tomatoes** (18 kg vs. 25 kg reorder level; 33.3% margin) and **Sukuma** (9 kg vs. 15 kg reorder level; 40.0% margin).
